3. Free trial, plans and billing

  • New shops get a free trial (currently 60 days). No card is needed to start.

  • We may offer free or courtesy access at our discretion, and we may end that access with notice.
  • If you pay by hand (for example Zelle or cash) instead of by card, your payment is due each month on the same day you started paying. The same 7-day grace period applies.

  • Everything you put in ColorBay (customers, vehicles, photos, estimates, invoices, payments, formulas and notes) belongs to you or your shop.
  • You give us permission to store and process it only to run the app for you.
  • You can ask for a full copy of your shop's data at any time, even if your account is paused or canceled.
  • We do not delete your data on our own. Items you delete go to the Trash and can be restored for 30 days; after that they are removed. We permanently delete a shop's data only when its owner asks us in writing.

When you save information about your own customers, your shop is responsible for having the right to collect and use it, and for following the laws that apply to you, including laws about text messages. Text messages from ColorBay open on your own phone and are sent by you. We handle your customers' information only on your behalf.

ColorBay is a tool. You are responsible for your prices, taxes, estimates, invoices, insurance claims and anything you send to customers or insurers. When a shop that signed up with your link makes 2 monthly payments, you get 1 free month: added to your trial if you're still on trial, applied as a credit to your next card payment, or your next month is not charged if you pay by hand. Free months have no cash value and can't be transferred.

Privacy Policy

In short: we collect what the app needs to work, we use it only to run ColorBay for you, we don't sell it or use it for ads, and you can ask for a copy or ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: the owner's email, shop name and phone number. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider; we can't see them.
  • Team: worker names and roles. PINs are stored scrambled (hashed), never as plain text.
  • Shop content you add: customer names, phone numbers and emails, vehicles and VINs, damage photos, signatures, estimates, invoices, payments, appointments, paint formulas and notes.
  • Agreement record: who accepted these Terms, which version, when, and the device type, so we can show it was accepted.
  • Billing: if you pay, Stripe tells us your plan and whether the payment went through. Card details stay with Stripe.
  • Referrals: if you signed up with a partner's or another shop's code or link, that person can see your shop's name and whether you're on trial, paying or paused. Nothing else.
  • Partners: for people who recommend ColorBay, we keep their name, contact, how we pay them, their signed agreement record and the tax forms the law requires (such as a W-9).
  • Technical: sign-in sessions and basic logs kept by our hosting providers for security and to keep the service running.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your shop between devices, keep your data safe, process payments, answer your questions, and tell you about important changes. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us run ColorBay

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the website files.
  • Stripe: payments, only if you subscribe.
  • NHTSA (U.S. Department of Transportation): receives only the VIN, and only when you tap "Read VIN".
  • Google Fonts: delivers the letter fonts the pages use.

These providers only get what they need to do their job. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety.

Your shop's customers

If you are a customer of a shop that uses ColorBay, that shop decides what information it keeps about you. Please contact the shop first. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to the shop and help it respond.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each shop can only reach its own information, photos are kept in private storage, and money details can be hidden from workers. No system is perfect; if a problem ever affects your data, we'll tell you promptly.

How long we keep it

We keep your shop's data while your account exists, and also after it is paused or canceled, so you never lose it, until the owner asks us to delete it. Items in the Trash are removed after 30 days.

Your choices

You can ask to see, download, correct or delete your information by contacting us. Owners can also correct most information directly in the app.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The app saves a few things on your device, such as your sign-in session, language and, in demo mode, the sample data.

Children

ColorBay is made for businesses and isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the version date, and the app asks everyone to review the new version.

  • Promotores: de las personas que recomiendan ColorBay guardamos su nombre, contacto, cómo les pagamos, el registro de su acuerdo firmado y los formularios de impuestos que pide la ley (como el W-9).
