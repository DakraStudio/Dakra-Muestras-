Our story

Every batch starts in a real kitchen

I'm Mary, and I've been baking for as long as I can remember. Where I grew up, cookies were how we said welcome, thank you and I'm proud of you.

Made By Mary H started at our garage sales, where neighbors kept coming back to ask for “more of those cookies.” Today I still bake in small batches, with real butter and good chocolate, the same way I bake for my own family.

Thank you for letting my kitchen be part of your table. Mary H.
